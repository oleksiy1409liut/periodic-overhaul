## Notes

- Ore blocks and ore chunks are not counted as elements on their own.
- Compounds such as oxides, salts, acids, alloys, and electronic components are not counted as separate elements.
- The representation column records the primary elemental definition identified; it does not list every compound or intermediate containing that element.
- Recheck this tracker against content/items/ and content/liquids/ whenever new elemental definitions are added.
